# Templates

## Thesis (`theses/TICKER.md`)
```markdown
# Thesis — X (Name)
Purchase date · Average price · Currency
## Why I bought (3 sentences)
## What must stay true (measurable: e.g. ROIC > 15%, growing dividend)
## Signals that break the thesis
## Buy price / estimated intrinsic value at the time
## Valuation assumptions (exact `calc.py dcf` command, date, pessimistic/base expected return)
## Horizon (by when the thesis must prove itself)
## Expectations for the next event (written before the results)
## Revisions (date — note)
```

## Report (committee meeting)
```markdown
# Investment Committee — <company/topic> — YYYY-MM-DD
## Decision
<BUY/WATCHLIST/PASS or recommendations per position> · Final score X/10 · Algorithm X/100 · Pessimistic/base expected return X%/Y% per year · Buy price · Current price · Confidence
## Summary (5 lines at most)
## Team opinions
### Fundamental analyst — X/10
… (one section per agent: score, 3 strengths, 3 risks, confidence, sources)
## Devil's advocate
## Data verification — verdict ✅/⚠️/❌ (corrected numbers and breaking point)
## Why the committee decided this way
---
This is not financial advice. Check the data before deciding.
```
